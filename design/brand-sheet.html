Plate 01 · construction

Everything that matters lives inside the safe circle

An adaptive icon is drawn on a 108 dp square, but the launcher masks it to a shape the app does not choose — a circle on one phone, a squircle on the next. Only the central 66 dp survives every mask. The flare reaches 27 units, the ring 32 along its long axis, and both companion stars ride the ring; nothing load‑bearing crosses the line.

The tilt is not a styling choice. A horizontal ring reads as a plate or a coin; at −22° it reads as a band of sky seen from underneath, which is the thing the app is actually about.

Plate 03 · themed icon

Tinted by the wallpaper, down to a silhouette

Android 13 and up throws the colour away and keeps the alpha. Gradients and glow do nothing here, so the flare is pulled in to radius 23 while the ring stays at full span — without that gap the two shapes fuse into a blob at 48 dp.

Plate 07 · palette

Nova's colours, not new ones

Lifted from the assistant's own interface rather than re-derived, so the icon, the app chrome and the deep-sky view are one palette. One honest cost: cyan is the worst of the bright hues for dark adaptation. That is why the app keeps a red night mode, and why the night mode was left alone.
